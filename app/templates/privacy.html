Privacy & data handling

ImportGuard is designed to run locally. This notice describes the current application behavior; it is not a legal compliance guarantee.

CSV uploads

The app parses bounded CSV uploads in memory and does not save uploaded source files as files on disk. Each validated run records an export entry (filename, row counts, recipe, report, and the ZIP package) in the server database for 7 days so you can restore downloads from history; entries are tied to your account or, for guests, to a random browser token. Deleting your account removes your entries. The app does not add analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. Your operating system, browser, or environment may have its own logging or storage behavior.

Optional Meta mapping

Manual mapping and local header suggestions do not contact Meta. The Meta action is disabled unless the server is configured with ALLOW_META_API_CALLS=true and a key. Invoking it sends source headers and target schema definitions to Meta's Model API. Example values are excluded by default and are sent only after a separate checkbox is selected; the full CSV is never sent for mapping. Meta processes information under its current privacy and legal terms. Review those terms before sending data.

Use synthetic data for demos

Do not upload sensitive or confidential information unless you are authorized to share it. Mapping suggestions may be wrong and must be reviewed. Validation checks only the configured rules; it does not guarantee data correctness.
